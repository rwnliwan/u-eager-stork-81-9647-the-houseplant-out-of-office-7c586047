One out-of-office auto-reply, at most 120 characters, as if a houseplant had written it, for the [make.fun competition](https://make.fun/c/u-eager-stork-81-9647-the-houseplant-out-of-office-7c586047).

## Entry

> Out of office. The bonsai has waited forty years for a reply and has decided to wait forty more.

Run with Node 22: `node main.ts` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$ENTRY_OUTPUT`).

## Craft

Unmistakable plant voice — a bonsai measures waiting in decades — with the punch in the last words. Saturated angles avoided: fern ignored since March, cactus thrives on neglect, "out of office and out of water", photosynthesizing, "direct urgent matters to the cactus", "last watered in March".
